Pichle 3 mahine mein teen bade AI labs ne ek hi cheez launch ki: **aisa AI jo aapke bolne ka wait nahi karta.** OpenAI ne Dots launch kiya, xAI ne Grok Bot, aur Anthropic ne Claude Duet.

Teeno ko "AI agent" bola ja raha hai. Par andar se teeno alag hain. Chaliye samajhte hain.

## Pehle samjho: chatbot aur agent mein fark kya hai?

Chatbot ek **receptionist** jaisa hai. Aap poochho, woh jawab de. Aap chup, woh chup.

Agent ek **personal assistant** jaisa hai. Usko kaam do, woh background mein karta rehta hai. Aapka laptop band ho tab bhi. Kaam ho gaya ya permission chahiye, toh khud message karta hai.

Teeno naye launches isi doosri category ke hain.

## 1. OpenAI Dots: ChatGPT ke andar "always-on" helper

- **Launch:** 29 September 2026, OpenAI DevDay par
- **Kya karta hai:** Aap ek goal dete ho, Dot uspe lagataar background mein kaam karta hai. Jaise customer feedback padhna aur bugs fix karna.
- **Kaise:** Har Dot ko apna cloud computer aur browser milta hai. 4,000 se zyada apps se jud sakta hai.
- **Kahan baat karo:** ChatGPT, Slack aur Microsoft Teams mein.
- **Safety:** Background research sirf "read-only" hoti hai. Message bhejna ya kuch badalna ho, toh aapki approval chahiye.
- **Kiske liye:** ChatGPT Pro aur Business Premium users. Pehla Dot plan mein included hai.

**Ek line mein:** Teams aur companies ke liye ek AI coworker jo ChatGPT ke ecosystem mein rehta hai.

## 2. GrokBot: "Job" wala bot, jo screen chalata hai

- **Launch:** 11 August 2026 (beta), SpaceXAI ki taraf se (pehle xAI). Official naam "Grok Bot" hai.
- **Kya karta hai:** Har Bot ka ek fixed role hota hai, jaise Sales Outbound, Talent Scout ya Expense Manager.
- **Khaas baat:** Apps aur websites ko **insaan ki tarah** chalata hai. Jis website ka API nahi hai, wahan bhi kaam kar leta hai.
- **Sikhaana aasan:** Aap ek baar kaam karke dikhao, Bot usko routine bana ke yaad rakhta hai.
- **Team of bots:** Kai Bots ek saath kaam karte hain, aur ek "Chief of Staff" Bot sabko coordinate karta hai.
- **Price:** Mehenga hai. SuperGrok Heavy ($300/month), Cursor Ultra ($200/month) ya Cursor Premium Teams ($120/seat) ke saath milta hai.

**Ek line mein:** Repetitive office kaam ke liye digital employees ki team.

## 3. Claude Duet: Aapke saare apps par nazar rakhne wala assistant

- **Launch:** 21 October 2026, research preview
- **Kya karta hai:** Subah ek short brief bhejta hai ki aaj kya important hai. Phir har kuch ghanton mein aapke apps check karta hai, aur **sirf tab message karta hai jab zaroori ho.**
- **Kaam:** Meeting schedule karne se lekar dashboard, sales forecast aur web app banane tak.
- **Connections:** Gmail, Slack, QuickBooks, Shopify, HubSpot, Canva jaise 6,000 se zyada connectors.
- **Safety:** Message bhejna, payment, document sign ya kuch delete karna ho, toh pehle dikhata hai aur approval maangta hai. Share trading aur accounts ke beech paisa bhejna allowed hi nahi hai.
- **Kiske liye:** Abhi sirf US ke Claude Pro aur Max users, waitlist ke through.

**Ek line mein:** Professionals aur small business owners ka proactive assistant.

## Teeno ek nazar mein

| | OpenAI Dots | GrokBot | Claude Duet |
|---|---|---|---|
| Soch | Goal do, background mein lage raho | Role do, app chalao | Nazar rakho, zaroorat pe bolo |
| Best for | Teams aur companies | Repetitive office kaam | Professionals, small business |
| Khaas power | Slack/Teams mein coworker | Bina API wali sites bhi | Morning brief + 6,000 connectors |
| Access | ChatGPT Pro/Business Premium | $120 se $300/month plans | US Pro/Max, waitlist |

## Hum Indians ke liye kya matlab?

Abhi teeno mein se koi bhi sasta ya har jagah available nahi hai. Par direction saaf hai: **2027 tak "AI se poochna" purana ho jayega, "AI ko kaam dena" naya normal hoga.**

Aaj kya karo? Agent ki soch abhi se seekho. Trigger, action aur approval. Yahi cheez N8N aur automation mein bhi hai. Jo ye samajh gaya, usko kisi bhi naye agent ko chalana 1 din ka kaam lagega.

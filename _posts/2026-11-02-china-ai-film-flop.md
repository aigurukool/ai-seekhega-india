**Socho zara.** Ek poori film. Har character, har scene, har background AI ne banaya. Pehle din 10,000 se zyada shows. Aur result? Har show mein average sirf 2 log.

Ye kahani hai China ki pehli full AI-generated film **"Sanxingdui: Future Past"** ki.

**Numbers dekho:**
- Budget: lagbhag 10 million yuan (₹12 crore ke aas-paas)
- Pehle weekend ki kamai: sirf $181K (₹1.5 crore approx)
- Team ne film ke liye 12 lakh se zyada AI images generate ki

**Aur ironic baat?** Film ki kahani hi ye hai ki ek superintelligent AI duniya pe kabza kar leta hai aur sab bigad jaata hai. AI ne AI ke khatre pe film banayi, aur audience ne kaha: "Nahi dekhni."

**Ab twist suno.** Isi saal China mein "Niu Lai" naam ki ek animated film aayi. Banane mein laga sirf $200. Quality itni kharab ki log mazaak udaane lage. Par us film ne $10 million kama liye!

Matlab kharab quality wali insaan ki film chal gayi, aur technically better AI film flop ho gayi.

**To kya AI films ka game khatam?** Bilkul nahi. China mein 2026 ki pehli timahi mein 95% se zyada microdramas (chhote online video serials) AI se bane. Chhote format mein AI chal raha hai, bade parde pe abhi nahi.

**Asli lesson kya hai?**
AI content ko sasta aur fast bana sakta hai. Par logon ko *feel* karwana, kisi kahani se jodna, ye abhi bhi insaan ka kaam hai.

Isliye agar aap creator ho, teacher ho, ya business chalate ho: AI ko apna **assistant** banao, apni jagah nahi. Idea, emotion aur kahani aapki. Speed aur scale AI ki.

Yahi combo jeetega. 🎬

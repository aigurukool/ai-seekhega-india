Sach bataun? Log AI automation seekhne mein fail isliye nahi hote ki ye mushkil hai. Fail isliye hote hain kyunki woh galat jagah se shuru karte hain. Seedha N8N kholte hain, 40 nodes dekhte hain, aur ghabra ke band kar dete hain.

Main 23 saal se logon ko training de raha hoon. Ek cheez pakki hai: **sahi sequence mein seekho, toh koi bhi skill aasan hai.** Ye raha AI automation ka roadmap.

## Step 1: Automation ki soch samjho (Week 1)

Har automation teen cheezon se banta hai:

- **Trigger:** kuch hua (form bhara gaya, email aaya)
- **Action:** kuch karna hai (sheet mein likho, WhatsApp bhejo)
- **Logic:** beech mein decision (agar ye, toh woh)

Bas itna. Apne roz ke kaam dekho aur likho: "Main kaun sa kaam baar baar karta hoon?" Wahi aapka pehla automation hai.

## Step 2: No-code tool se shuru karo (Week 2)

Zapier ya Make.com lo. Free plan kaafi hai. Pehla project:

- Google Form bhara jaaye, toh Google Sheet mein entry aur aapko email

Ye chhota lagta hai, par trigger aur action ka asli matlab yahin samajh aata hai.

## Step 3: AI ko jodo (Week 3)

Ab automation mein dimaag daalo. ChatGPT, Gemini ya Claude ko apne workflow ka hissa banao:

- Customer ka message aaya, AI ne reply draft kiya
- Lambi email aayi, AI ne 3 line ka summary diya

Yahan **prompt likhna** sabse important skill hai. Clear instruction, ek example, aur output ka format. Itna yaad rakho.

## Step 4: N8N par aao (Week 4 aur 5)

Jab Zapier mehenga lagne lage, N8N aapka dost hai. Self-host karo, toh lagbhag free.

- Webhook samjho: bahar se data andar kaise aata hai
- Google Sheets ko apna chhota database banao
- HTTP Request node seekho: isse koi bhi app jud jaati hai

Shuru mein errors aayenge. Ghabrana nahi. Har error kuch sikhata hai.

## Step 5: APIs se dosti karo (Week 6)

API matlab ek app ka doosri app se baat karne ka tareeka. Teen cheezein samajh lo:

- **Endpoint:** kahan request bhejni hai
- **API key:** aapki pehchaan
- **JSON:** data ka format

Ye aa gaya, toh aap kisi bhi tool ko automate kar sakte ho.

## Step 6: Asli project banao (Week 7 aur 8)

Course dekhne se nahi, banane se aata hai. Inmein se ek banao:

- Instagram comment par auto reply bot
- Google Review maangne ka QR system
- WhatsApp par lead aaye toh sheet mein entry aur follow-up

Ek project poora karo, aadhe-adhoore paanch se behtar hai.

## Last baat

AI automation ab "nice to have" skill nahi rahi. Jo aaj seekh raha hai, kal woh team ka sabse valuable insaan hoga. 8 hafte do, roz 1 ghanta. Bas itna chahiye.

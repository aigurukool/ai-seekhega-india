**Jis insaan ne NVIDIA ka AI empire banaya, wahi chale gaye.**

Jensen Huang ne NVIDIA ke CEO pad se resign kar diya. AI industry ka sabse bada leadership shake-up. Ab NVIDIA ka kya hoga?

**Kya hua?**

12 November 2026 ko Jensen Huang ne NVIDIA ke President, CEO aur Board member pad se turant resign kar diya.

1993 mein unhone NVIDIA co-found kiya tha aur ek graphics chip company ko duniya ka AI computing powerhouse bana diya.

**Resign kyun kiya?**

Unke personal relationships ko lekar reports aayi, jisme ek businesswoman bhi shaamil hai jise US authorities Chinese intelligence se juda maanti hain. NVIDIA ke board ne independent investigation shuru ki, controversy badhti gayi, aur Huang ko pad chhodna pada.

**Naye CEO kaun?**

Colette Kress, jo 2013 se NVIDIA ki CFO hain, ab **interim CEO** hain. Woh CFO bhi bani rahengi.

- NVIDIA se pehle Cisco aur Microsoft mein senior finance roles
- NVIDIA ki financial growth sambhalne mein badi bhoomika
- Saath hi Harvey C. Jones NVIDIA ke pehle Board Chair bane

Dhyaan rahe: permanent CEO abhi announce nahi hua hai.

**Jensen ki legacy**

33 saal, ek zabardast transformation. GPUs, CUDA, AI data centres aur accelerated computing: aaj ka AI unhi ki neev par khada hai. Woh ek aisi company chhod kar ja rahe hain jo global AI revolution ke centre mein hai.

**NVIDIA ke liye iska matlab?**

Technology wahi hai, leadership badli hai. Product roadmap aur customer commitments chalte rahenge. Par investors in sawaalon par nazar rakhenge:

- Permanent CEO kaun banega?
- Kya AI strategy same rahegi?
- Kya Jensen ke bina execution ho payega?
- Governments aur regulators ke saath rishta badlega?

**Hamare liye iska matlab?**

Kya AI mehnga hoga? Kya ChatGPT badlega? **Abhi nahi.**

OpenAI, Google, Microsoft sab NVIDIA ke GPUs aur infrastructure use karte hain. Leadership change se aage AI hardware, pricing aur supply ki direction badal sakti hai. Par NVIDIA ki technology, CUDA ecosystem aur existing infrastructure raaton-raat gayab nahi honge.

Asli sawaal yeh hai: AI computing ke agle phase ko lead kaun karega?

**Big Takeaway**

Ek CEO gaya, AI race jaari hai. Jensen Huang ka resignation NVIDIA ke ek shandaar chapter ka ant hai. Par AI revolution kisi ek insaan se kahin bada hai.
